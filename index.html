A little more confidence. A little more you.

Established in 2019, Benisse Studio is a lash business dedicated to helping you feel like yourself, only a little more luminous.

We take a personal, considered approach to every set, creating a look that complements your features, eye shape, and everyday life.

From soft, effortless classics to wispy, textured styles and customized lash designs, every detail is thoughtfully considered to celebrate your natural beauty.
